# Social & Ethical Aspects of AI

## Spring 2027 - University of Wisconsin-Madison

---

This course provides an overview of how AI intersects with social behavior and society. Students will learn how AI can be used to answer social scientific questions, and they will also engage with ethical and social issues across the model development pipeline. Lectures and discussions will draw from fields such as computational social science, cultural analytics, responsible AI, AI safety, and HCI.
